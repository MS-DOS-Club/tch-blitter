# Recording what is learned about the hardware

## One source, one projection

`hardware/*.yaml` is **the data**: `registers.yaml`, `symbols.yaml`, `accesses.yaml`, and
`gfx.yaml` (the blit command as data) and `gfx-rltennis.yaml` (Reality Tennis's format, asset and
RAMDAC). The Markdown under
`docs/boards/` **and `disasm/symbols.txt`** are its **render**, generated by `make hw-docs`.
`radendur-v2.yaml` is not board data and nothing projects it: it is the patch table
`scripts/radendur-v2.py` builds the Radical Enduro v2 ROM from.
A block marked as generated is never edited by hand: the next run overwrites it.

The prose around those blocks is written by hand and lives in the same file: the narrative (how
something was measured, why a hypothesis failed) is prose, and the tabular part (registers, bits,
addresses, ranges) is data.

## The order of work, on confirming or refuting something

1. **The YAML first**, with its evidence, when it is established: what is not written down is lost.
2. **`make hw-docs`** to project.
3. Only then carry on.

## What the schema requires, and why

Field names are the English ones below. A key the schema does not know is ignored and the datum is
lost in silence, which is worse than a hard error.

- `state: confirmed` requires `evidence`.
- `state: refuted` requires `symptom`: a refutation without the symptom that killed it saves nobody
  the work.
- `state: open` accepts `observed`: what was measured, per machine, **uninterpreted**. Counts and
  ranges go there, which is what tells a coordinate bit from a mode bit.
- `polarity` is its own field: a mechanism can be right with its sense inverted.
- A blitter bit that is a field of the blit command carries `field:` (its name in
  `hardware/gfx.yaml`) and no `mask`; why, and what is refused, is `gen-hw-docs.py`'s
  `resolve_fields`.
- `level` says what can be trusted, and it is two vocabularies because they are two different
  questions:
  - in `games.yaml`, where the datum comes from: `verified` (read from a binary or from MAME's code,
    with `evidence` re-checkable today), `external` (from an outside source, with `sources`, and not
    promoted for sounding plausible) and `unknown`, which is said rather than filled in;
  - in `symbols.yaml`, how the symbol was established: `verified`, `correspondence` (located by flow
    correspondence with the sibling and confirmed instruction by instruction) and `identical` (256
    equal bytes). These are values, not prose.

`gen-hw-docs.py` refuses a schema that breaks these rules, and its `--check` (in `make check`) says
whether the Markdown still matches the YAML.
